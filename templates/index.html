How a review gets scored

From raw text to a verdict you can trust

01

Read the language

Every review is cleaned and vectorised, then scored for sentiment in English and Bahasa Melayu — including local negators like "tak" and "bukan" that plain English tools miss entirely.

02

Check the behaviour

Beyond the words: is this exact text duplicated elsewhere? Did the product just get a burst of reviews overnight? Behavioural flags catch what wording alone can't.

03

Explain the verdict

Two models vote on a final score, then every triggered signal is laid out plainly — so the result is never a black box, even to someone outside ML.
